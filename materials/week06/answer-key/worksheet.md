### Part B

1. **Word processor.** Writing and editing text documents.
2. **Media player.** Playing video (or audio) files.
3. **Web browser.** Viewing pages on the internet.
4. **Photo editor.** Viewing and editing pictures.
5. **Messaging app.** Sending texts, photos, or calls.
6. **Game.** Entertainment the user directly controls.
