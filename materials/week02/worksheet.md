**3rd** = integrated circuits · **4th** = microprocessors ·

   Generation: ______  Why: ______________________________________

   Generation: ______  Why: ______________________________________

   Generation: ______  Why: ______________________________________

6. **A computer fills a whole room and often overheats.**
   Generation: ______  Why: ______________________________________
