## Professor Answer Key — do not hand out this section

Accept any reasonable, well-explained answer. Multiple ideas are often
defensible at once; the goal is reasoning, not a single "correct" pick.

### Part A

1. **IoT.** A thermostat with a sensor and its own small chip is a
   connected, smart device.
2. **Cloud.** The photo synced through a distant server; no cable, no
   local network needed.
3. **Neither.** A basic calculator follows a fixed rule, with no
   sensor or connection involved.
4. **Cloud.** The video is stored and streamed from a server, not
   from the device itself.
5. **IoT.** A tracker with its own sensor works even without a nearby
   phone.
6. **Neither.** A paper notebook has no chip, no sensor, and no
   connection at all.

### Part B

1. **IoT** (the speaker senses and acts) and **Cloud** (it usually
   asks a distant server to understand your voice). Accept either, or both.
2. **Mobile computing.** A phone doing heavy graphics work is a full,
   small computer.
3. **Big data.** Studying billions of searches needs many computers,
   working together.
4. **Cloud.** Seeing the same photos from any device is a classic
   cloud sync example.
5. **IoT** (a location sensor) and **Mobile computing** (a phone app
   tracking it). Accept either, or both.
6. **Big data.** Millions of past trips is far too much data for one
   computer to study alone.
