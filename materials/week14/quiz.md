# Week 14 Self-Check Quiz

Introduction to Computer Engineering (400507-001) · Week 14
This quiz is not graded. It checks what you remember. Try it alone,
without your notes. About 10 minutes.

---

1. Which one best describes **IoT (Internet of Things)**?
   a) Software that follows only rules a person wrote by hand
   b) Everyday objects that connect to the internet
   c) A type of programming language
   d) A very large data center

2. What does **cloud computing** actually mean?
   a) Data stored in the sky, above the clouds
   b) Using computer power and storage over the internet, on someone else's server
   c) A wireless connection with no server involved
   d) A backup copy saved only on your own device

3. Why do people call something **big data**?
   a) The files use a large font size
   b) The amount of data is too large for one computer to hold or study alone
   c) It only means old, outdated data
   d) It is data used only by governments

4. Jin's smart watch counts his steps, even when his phone is left in
   another room. What is the **best** explanation?
   a) The watch is broken and needs a repair
   b) The watch is its own small, connected computer, with its own sensor
   c) The watch is secretly connected by a hidden cable
   d) Step counting always needs a nearby phone

5. What best describes **mobile computing**?
   a) A phone that can only make calls, nothing else
   b) A full computer, built small enough to carry
   c) A computer that never has a CPU
   d) Any device that cannot connect to the internet

6. True or false: "IoT, cloud computing, big data, and mobile
   computing each need a brand-new, seventh layer, not in Week 1's picture."

7. Put these data sizes in order, smallest to largest: **gigabyte,
   kilobyte, terabyte, petabyte**.

8. **Short answer.** In two sentences or less, explain one real
   tradeoff that comes with using cloud computing or smart devices.

9. Which three signs together are used to describe **big data**?
   a) Size, price, and speed
   b) Volume, variety, and velocity
   c) Hardware, software, and network
   d) Cost, quality, and time

10. What does **3D printing (additive manufacturing)** do?
    a) Cuts a shape out of a larger block of material
    b) Builds an object by adding material, layer by layer
    c) Only prints flat paper documents
    d) Melts an object down into raw material

11. Why does a self-driving car use **sensor fusion**, combining a
    camera and radar together?
    a) Cameras and radar always show the exact same information
    b) So one sensor's weakness, like camera glare, does not make the car fail alone
    c) Radar is required by law, even if not useful
    d) Combining sensors makes the car lighter

12. True or false: "A cryptocurrency and an NFT are exactly the same
    kind of blockchain record."

13. What does the **Fourth Industrial Revolution (4IR)** describe?
    a) The fourth time computers were invented
    b) A wave of change where physical machines, digital computing, and living systems increasingly work together
    c) A new programming language
    d) A rule that only applies to smartphones
