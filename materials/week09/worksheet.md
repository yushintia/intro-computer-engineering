# Week 9 Worksheet: Two Laptops, One Meter Apart

Introduction to Computer Engineering (400507-001) · Week 9
Work with a partner. There can be more than one correct answer.

Running story: two students sit side by side. One has a photo on her
screen. The other wants a copy, right now. There is no wire between
the two laptops. This worksheet builds the words you need to explain
how the photo could actually get across.

### C2. Order the pipeline

your laptop out to the wider internet. Number them 1 to 4.

- ____ Router (directs data toward the right address)
- ____ NIC (joins your laptop to the network)
- ____ Modem (converts data for the ISP's line)
- ____ Hub (shares the wired connection locally)

### C3. Getting online
