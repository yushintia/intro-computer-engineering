## Instructor Answer Key — do not hand out this section

Accept any reasonable, well-explained answer. The goal is reasoning,
not a single "correct" pick.

### Part A

1. **Not AI.** A clock just displays time; it follows one fixed rule, no learning involved.
2. **AI.** Sorting photos by face uses a trained model to recognize patterns.
3. **Not AI.** Adding two numbers is a fixed rule, not a learned pattern.
4. **AI.** Predicting the next word is a trained model's guess, based on many examples.
5. **AI.** Recommending content is a trained model's prediction about what you might like.
6. **Not AI.** Counting seconds from a button press is a fixed rule, not a prediction.

### Part B

1. **Bias.** One student failing consistently, while most others succeed, points to unfair or incomplete training data for that student's face.
2. **Error.** One single wrong guess, without a pattern, is a normal, isolated error.
3. **Bias.** A consistent gap between one accent and another points to unequal training data.
4. **Neither.** The filter did its job correctly; this is the tool working as expected.
5. **Bias.** Training mostly on one group's past resumes teaches the model that group's patterns best, at the cost of fairness to others.
6. **Error.** One uncommon word failing once, without a consistent group pattern, is a normal, isolated error.
