# Week 13 Self-Check Quiz

Introduction to Computer Engineering (400507-001) · Week 13
This quiz is not graded. It checks what you remember. Try it alone,
without your notes. About 10 minutes. This is not Quiz 2.

---

1. Which one best describes **artificial intelligence (AI)**?
   a) Software that follows only rules a person wrote by hand
   b) Software that learns patterns from examples
   c) Any software that runs on a phone
   d) A type of computer hardware

2. What does **training** mean, in AI?
   a) Deleting old data from a computer
   b) Showing a computer many labeled examples, so it can learn
   c) Restarting a frozen app
   d) Connecting a device to wifi

3. What is a **model**, after training is complete?
   a) A single labeled example
   b) The learned pattern an AI built from its training examples
   c) A picture used only for testing
   d) The programmer who wrote the software

4. A phone's photo app sorts a dog's photo into a person's folder by
   mistake. What is the **best** explanation?
   a) The app is broken and needs to be reinstalled
   b) The model made a wrong best guess, a normal AI error
   c) The dog secretly has a person's face
   d) The photo app is not really using AI

5. What is **bias**, in an AI model's training data?
   a) When training data is unfair or incomplete, so guesses are less fair too
   b) When a model runs too slowly
   c) When a photo is blurry
   d) When a phone's battery is low

6. True or false: "A face-unlock feature failing more often for one
   group of people is usually just random bad luck."

7. Put these in order: **prediction, labeled examples, training, model**.

8. **Short answer.** In two sentences or less, explain why an AI
   model can still guess wrong on a photo it has never seen before.
