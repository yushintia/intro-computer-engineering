## Chain (Limits -> Pain), see SPINE.md for full text

1 -> 2 -> 3 -> 4 -> 5 -> 6 -> 7 -> (8 review) -> 9 -> 10 -> 11 -> 12 -> 13
-> 14 -> (15 review). The semester walks outward from a single device:
history, then logic, then the processor, then memory, then software,
then the operating system, then networking, then programming, then
data and security, then a broader survey, then AI, then the future.

## Running case study

"What's Actually Inside Your Laptop": the student's own everyday device
(laptop or phone) is opened up conceptually, one layer at a time, across
the semester. Week 1 sketches the whole layered picture in one pass;
each later week zooms into exactly one layer (history, logic, CPU,
memory, software, OS, networking, programming, databases, AI) with the
same device as the running worked example.
